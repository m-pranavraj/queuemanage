Product Requirements Document (PRD) ZP CEO Appointment & Visitor Intelligence Portal Working Name: ZP Sampark Users: Citizens / Visitors, CEO Office, ZP CEO / IAS Officer, Admin Platform: Mobile-first Web Application Primary Entry: QR Code + Web Link 1. Problem Statement The Zilla Parishad CEO's office receives a large number of visitors seeking meetings for grievances, proposals, approvals, departmental issues and other matters. The current walk-in process creates four problems: 1. 2. 3. 4. Visitors do not know when they will get an opportunity to meet the CEO. The CEO's office has limited visibility into who is waiting and why. The CEO cannot effectively prioritise or manage the live queue. Valuable information about recurring citizen issues is lost because visitor data is not systematically captured and analysed. The proposed platform will digitise the complete journey: QR Scan → Visitor Registration → Appointment/Visit Request → Screening → Queue → Meeting → Action/Referral → Analytics 2. Product Objective Create a simple digital visitor management system through which: Citizen Can scan a QR code, register the purpose of the visit and receive a digital token/appointment status. CEO Office Can review visitors, manage appointments and operate the live queue. 1ZP CEO Can see exactly: • • • • • • Who is waiting? Why have they come? How long have they waited? Who should be called next? Which department does the matter concern? What happened after the meeting? Administration Can analyse: • • • • • • What issues are citizens approaching the CEO for? Which departments generate the most grievances? Which talukas/areas generate the most cases? How many cases are repeat visits? What is the average waiting time? How many matters are resolved/referred/pending? 3. Success Metrics Within the first 90 days, track: Metric Target Visitors digitally registered >90% Average registration time <2 minutes Queue visibility 100% Average waiting time Reduce by 30% Meetings with captured outcome >90% Department referrals digitally tracked >80% Repeat unresolved visits Reduce month-on-month CEO dashboard adoption Daily 1626. Product Principle The portal should optimise for: Citizen convenience + CEO's time + administrative intelligence. The citizen interface should therefore be extremely simple. The CEO interface should be even simpler. The officer should ideally be able to operate the entire queue through: Call Next → Read Brief → Meet → Refer/Resolve → Next with each interaction requiring only a few clicks.
