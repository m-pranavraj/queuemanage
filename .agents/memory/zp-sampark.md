# ZP Sampark visual direction

The portal is an India-focused public-service experience for a Zilla Parishad CEO / IAS Officer office.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested national flag colours and no purple or violet.

**How to apply:** Keep future ZP Sampark screens in saffron, white, green, and restrained navy/ink; preserve a dignified, accessible civic tone across citizen and office workflows.
